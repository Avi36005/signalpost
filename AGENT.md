# Agent policy

`aviscope.runner` reads the official batch file (JSONL, JSON array, CSV or one number per line) and validates nine-digit Norwegian organisation numbers, including their checksum. Any batch size is accepted. The stable key is the legal organisation number, never a brand or name.

Every company first receives its official baseline: exact registry identity, registered homepage and head-office address, latest available annual accounts, roles, subunits and group structure. Website research for a company starts as soon as its baseline lands and runs on other hosts in parallel, so it never delays another company's official reads. A supplied frozen registry replaces live identity; an absent frozen row fails that company without publishing live replacements. `--history` adds rate-limited filing-year discovery after website work, and skips it when its queue is long. JSON pointers bind published values to retained evidence.

Website candidates come from the registry homepage or, when none is declared, at most two DNS-resolving domains spelling the exact legal name. Robots, SSRF-safe DNS pinning and budgets apply to every fetch. The tiered identity gate (`IDENTITY_RESOLUTION.md`) accepts an organisation number on the page, a registry-declared homepage showing the exact legal name, or a registry-declared domain spelling the legal name. It abstains on competing checksum-valid numbers, shared portals, portfolio/group wording, other legal names and parked pages. Two same-host contact/about pages may independently prove an inconclusive home page. A verified host's careers/news/contact pages inherit host ownership only. JSON-LD jobs and articles are rejected when their employer or subject names a different entity. Linked profiles must spell the company and are kept at one per platform.

The frozen routing choices are fixed observed-link order (the default) and gap-directed priority. No thresholds learn from hidden daily results.

Synthesis is a deterministic template over supported, undisputed claims. Each sentence cites its claim ids and unknown families are listed explicitly. No LLM is enabled. A page's instructions are inert text, cannot invoke tools and cannot override identity gates. Missing jobs never imply no hiring. Promotional claims do not become independent sentiment.
