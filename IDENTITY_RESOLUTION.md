# Exact entity publication

Official records are addressed by the input organisation number. Registry and annual-account response identifiers must equal that number. Subunit `overordnetEnhet` must equal it; the subunit's own number stays separate. Company roles are scoped to the public exact-entity role endpoint. Group responses remain group-scoped claims and never populate subsidiary financial metrics.

External website publication (October 14 policy) uses ordered evidence tiers. Every tier also requires: no other checksum-valid organisation number on the page, no parked-domain marker, and a host that is not a known shared portal or site builder (`SHARED_HOSTS`, e.g. OBOS/USBL housing portals, shopping-centre sites, Wix/Squarespace subdomains, directories).

1. **Exact organisation number on the page.** Registry-declared domains accept it directly. A name-derived (guessed) domain also needs the domain to spell the legal name or the page to show the exact legal name. The number shown under a *different* legal name (franchise, client list) is not accepted.
2. **Registry-declared homepage showing the exact legal name** (for example `JAKT OG FJELLSPORT AS`). Not accepted on name alone when the page names other legal entities (`... AS`) or uses portfolio/group/customer-list wording, because that is the shape of a parent's, investor's or supplier's site.
3. **Registry-declared homepage whose own registrable domain spells the exact legal name** (`kongsberg-regnskapstjenester.no` for `KONGSBERG REGNSKAPSTJENESTER AS`). The company itself declared this homepage to Brønnøysundregistrene; the name-spelling domain shows it is not a shared portal.

Name-derived domains (`www.<legal-name>.no/.com`, only when the registry lists no website) are discovery candidates. DNS is checked first, so nonexistent guesses cost no HTTP request. They publish only through tier 1, or the exact legal name on a domain spelling it. Inconclusive home pages may lead to two same-host contact/about/privacy pages, each gated independently.

After a host is verified, up to five observed same-host pages (careers, news, contact, about) are read. A page on the verified host inherits host-level ownership only. It never adds the home description again, and any page carrying another organisation number publishes nothing. From verified pages AviScope publishes: `og:site_name` brand, linked company profiles on known platforms (link URLs only; Facebook/LinkedIn/Instagram/X/YouTube/TikTok are never fetched; share/intent/person links are rejected), JSON-LD `JobPosting` (rejected when `hiringOrganization` names a different taxID or a different name) and JSON-LD articles with headline and publication date. The registry's own `hjemmeside` value is also kept as the official `registered_homepage` fact.

Adversarial tests cover name-only matches, franchise names, parent/shared identifiers, portfolio mentions, parked pages and numbers assembled from unrelated text. Compared with the starter's name-token threshold, these cases exercise stricter rejection, not independent quality measurement.

Identity links contain endpoints/domain, relationship type, status, source snapshot/locator or evidence IDs, and reasons. Rejected candidates stay in `candidate_ledger`. Roles and locations never get inferred from a brand's group site. Unresolved same-period/scope values enter `review_queue`; different periods and group/entity scopes are separate keys.

September 14 update: an inconclusive home page with no conflicting organisation identifiers may lead to at most two observed same-host contact links. Each page must independently pass the same exact-entity gate; contact-page proof never authorizes home-page claims. Subsequent pages no longer create conflicting official-website URLs. Structured claims have precise JSON-LD array/graph locators; the integrity auditor now checks HTML organisation spans, descriptions and structured values, including tampering regressions. Live improvement must be measured separately.
