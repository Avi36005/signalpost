- Registry-linked company-owned websites are researched by default (`SOURCE_RIGHTS.md`); only named third-party platforms remain hard-disabled. Despite this, a live 100-company sample found only 6 companies with any registry-listed website, and none of those 6 candidates passed the exact-entity identity gate (2 blocked by robots.txt/redirects, 4 correctly quarantined as ambiguous shared-portal/group/SPV sites) — so website, brand, company profiles, jobs and public activity were still usually unavailable in that sample. The official-data corpus is researched but partial. Do not claim competitive external coverage or that the entry qualifies; see `out/live-100-external/run-report.json` and the full-corpus run for measured rates.
- Search, PDF financial extraction, JavaScript rendering, sentiment and paid integrations are disabled. Filed numeric accounts and optionally available historical filing years are supported; historical PDF values are not fabricated.
- Strict website identity can abstain on legitimate sites, especially groups/shared domains. Exact external precision and recall are unmeasured without independent reviewed labels. The organizer's pooled reference is unavailable.
- Gap-directed routing exists but a measured fixed-vs-gap comparison with external domains enabled (`reports/evaluation/routing-comparison.json`) showed no claim-count difference on the tested cohort. Fixed routing remains the frozen default. Do not call heuristic priority weights official scoring weights.
- Roles/subunits rely on official exact-entity endpoints; group evidence is explicitly group-scoped. The subunit route reads at most 1,000 records and flags further pages. No shareholder or beneficial-owner enrichment is enabled.
- Refresh retains missing records as stale and never confirms removals from disappearance alone. It does not currently implement confirmed closed-job events. Official 410 removal needs review of historical artifacts before external distribution.
