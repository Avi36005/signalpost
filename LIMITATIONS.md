# Known limitations and readiness blockers

- **External recall is still the weak dimension.** Most companies in the eligible universe are small and declare no website to the registry (about 12% do in our 1,000-company sample). AviScope now also tries domains spelling the exact legal name, but without a search API most companies still have no discoverable, provably owned site. Website, brand, linked profiles, jobs and public activity therefore remain `not_available` for most profiles. That state means "not found from permitted exact-entity sources", not "does not exist". Measured rates are in `EVAL.md`.
- **Identity precision is designed, not independently measured.** Since November 2026 every tier needs the page to show the exact legal name or the organisation number; a matching host alone no longer passes. Tiers 2 and 3 accept the exact legal name without the number. They abstain on shared portals, portfolio/group wording, other legal names and competing checksum-valid numbers, but a parent's site that prints the subsidiary's exact legal name, with no other entity named, could still pass. Every accepted external claim in the 100-company smoke test was reviewed manually; no organizer gold set or independent labels exist.
- **Linked profiles are company-declared links, not visits.** Profile URLs (Facebook, LinkedIn, Instagram, X, YouTube, TikTok, Pinterest, Vimeo, GitHub) are published only when linked from a verified company site and when the handle spells the legal name, brand or domain. The platforms themselves are never fetched, so a stale or deleted profile is not detected.
- **Jobs and activity only come from company-owned JSON-LD.** Career pages hosted on external applicant-tracking systems, plain-HTML job lists and news without JSON-LD are not extracted. NAV arbeidsplassen ads carry the employer `orgnr` and would enable exact matching. Its `pam-stilling-feed` is free but needs a bearer token: the public token is for experiments only, and production use needs a written registration with NAV, which has not been done. The feed has no per-employer query, so it would have to be mirrored in full. It stays disabled (`docs/NOV2026_CHANGE_PLAN.md`).
- **No independent news.** Searched news is not enabled. GDELT DOC 2.0 is free but returned HTTP 429 to 11 of 12 polite test queries on 2026-11-06 and gives metadata only; licensed feeds are paid and need approval. `public_activity` therefore comes only from JSON-LD articles on verified company sites.
- **Disabled:** search APIs, browser rendering (JavaScript-only sites look empty), historical PDF financial extraction, sentiment and LLMs. LLM use is now allowed by the contract with a Builderr-supplied key; none is used, so `expected_cost_per_official_run_usd` is 0.
- **Filing-year history is best-effort.** It is limited to one start per 2.1 s and is skipped for a company when that queue exceeds 20 s, so in large batches only some companies get `available_filing_year` facts. Account records from the numeric API are unaffected.
- **Time budget is not published.** Defaults are 40 requests per company and max(2,600 s, 2.7 s per company). Registry pacing (0.18 s per start) needs about 15–16 minutes per 1,000 companies for the official baseline. Set `--max-seconds` below the evaluator limit; when the deadline arrives, optional work stops and every company still gets an envelope.
- **Refresh** retains missing records as stale and never confirms removals from disappearance alone. Closed-job events are not implemented.
- **Local resource measurements** come from this macOS/Windows development hardware, not the organizer's environment.
- **Starter licence.** The upstream starter has no explicit LICENSE file. Redistribution terms remain to be confirmed with Builderr.
- **Hosting.** The agent and its research workbench run locally only. The public JBox website (`docs/JBOX_BUILD_PROVENANCE.md`) is a read-only view of the October 100-company snapshot, not live data. It does not reflect November agent changes until a new snapshot is exported. JBox hosting costs about 1 credit per day.
- **The 108-of-300 observation is unexplained.** The organiser's command, commit and logs are not available; a clarification is drafted. The deadline-safe runner and the 100/300/1,000/1,100 fixtures test our own runs, not theirs.

Passing fixtures and integrity audits does not measure independent company-match accuracy or official qualification.
